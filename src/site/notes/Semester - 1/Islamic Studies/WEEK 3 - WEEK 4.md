# WEEK 3

## Quran Studies : Uloom ul Quran & Textual Codification

A comprehensive analysis of basic themes, sciences of revelation & historical stages of compilation with Quran & hadith existance

> [!question] Definition & Linguistic Etymology of the Quran
> LInguistic Origin -> Derived from the root word Qara'a meaning "to read" or "recite frequently".
> Technical Terminology -> Literal speech of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad (SAWW) via Angel JIbril AS

> [!NOTE] Five Primary Themes of the Quranic DIscourse 
> 1. Taweed -> Absolute oneness, sovereignty, & divine attributes of Allah.
> 2. Risalah -> Prophethood, divine guidance, & finality of Muhammad (SAWW)
> 3. Akhirah -> Resurrection, accountability, divine justice, & eternal reward
> 4. Ahkam -> Legal frameworks, moral codes, & social regulations
> 5. Qasas -> Historical narratives of past nations & prophets for moral lessons

## Stages of Preservation & Codification

> [!NOTE] Stages
> 1. **Prophetic Era (610–632 CE)**
>     
>     - **Oral**: Memorized by Sahabah; annual Ramadan review with Angel Jibril AS (_Bukhari 4998_).
>         
>     - **Written**: Recorded on parchments, stones, and leather by scribes like Zayd bin Thabit RA (scattered status).
>         
> 2. **Caliphate of Abu Bakr RA (632–634 CE) — First Compilation**
>     
>     - **Cause**: ~70 _Huffaz_ martyred at Battle of Yamama.
>         
>     - **Execution**: Zayd bin Thabit RA bound scattered media into one master volume (_Suhuf_) using strict 2-witness verification (_Bukhari 4986_).
>         
> 3. **Caliphate of Uthman RA (644–656 CE) — Standardization**
>     
>     - **Cause**: Regional dialect disputes during military expansion (_Bukhari 4987_).
>         
>     - **Execution**: Master copy retrieved from Hafsah RA; copied in the **Quraishi dialect** (_Mushaf-e-Uthmani_), sent to major provinces, and private copies destroyed.
>         
> 4. **Post-Caliphate Script Evolution**
>     
>     - **Dotting (_I'jam_)**: Added by Abu al-Aswad al-Du'ali (d. 688 CE) to distinguish letters ($ب, ت, ث$).
>         
>     - **Diacritics (_Tashkeel_)**: Added by Al-Khalil ibn Ahmad al-Farahidi (d. 786 CE) for short vowel marks (_Fatha, Kasra, Damma_).
> 

### Abu Bakr (RA) vs. Uthman (RA) Compilations

| **Aspect**  | **Abu Bakr (RA)**                         | **Uthman (RA)**                                |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Trigger** | Loss of _Huffaz_ at Yamama                | Dialect conflicts in expanded empire           |
| **Goal**    | **Gather** scattered text into one volume | **Standardize** to a single dialect (Quraish)  |
| **Result**  | One central master manuscript (_Suhuf_)   | Official codices (_Masahif_) sent to provinces |
