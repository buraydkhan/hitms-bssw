## UNDERSTANDING THE BASIC CONCEPT OF RELIGION 

> [!NOTE] DEEN AND RELIGION FROM A ISLAMIC POV
> - **Religion (Madhhab / Specialized Practice):** In Western discourse, "religion" often refers to personal faith, rituals, and acts of worship (like prayer and fasting) separate from public or civic life.
> - **Deen (Complete Way of Life):** _Deen_ encompasses all aspects of existence. It includes theology (Akida), acts of worship (Ibadah), ethics (Akhlaq), laws (Sharia), business conduct (Muamalat), and social governance.

> [!question] What is Religion?
>  Religion is a system of beliefs and practices that connect human beings with God and provides guidance for life.
> Provides a framework for understanding God, life and human responsibilites. Guidance for individual and social behaviour. Gives directions for moral and spiritual.

> [!NOTE] What it gives you :
> LIfe -> Purpose and meaning
> Moral guidance -> right and wrong 
> Spiritual -> Satisfaction and hope
> Guidance -> personal and social life
> Sense of accountibility

## PROPHETS : 

> [!NOTE] Prophets :
> - **Prophet Musa (Moses) — Given the Tawrat**
>     
>     - **Staff Turning into a Serpent:** His staff transformed into a massive real serpent, swallowing the illusions of Pharaoh's greatest magicians.
>         
>     - **Parting of the Red Sea:** Struck the sea with his staff, splitting the waters to create a safe path for the Children of Israel to escape Pharaoh's army.
>         
>     - **The Radiant Hand (_Yad-e-Bayda_):** Inserting his hand into his cloak produced a brilliant, shining white light without any defect or harm.
>         
>     - **Water from the Rock:** Striking a rock with his staff caused twelve springs to gush forth for the twelve tribes of Israel.
>         
> - **Prophet Dawud (David) — Given the Zabur**
>     
>     - **Softening of Iron:** Allah made solid iron pliable like wax in his hands without requiring fire or tools, allowing him to craft advanced chainmail armor.
>         
>     - **Melodious Voice & Creation's Praise:** When he recited the Zabur, mountains and birds were commanded to echo and join in his praise of Allah.
>         
>     - **Defeat of Jalut (Goliath):** Defeated the formidable tyrant Jalut using only a simple sling and stone when he was a young soldier.
>         
> - **Prophet Isa (Jesus) — Given the Injeel**
>     
>     - **Speaking in Infancy:** Spoke clearly as a newborn from the cradle to defend his mother Maryam (Mary) and declare his prophethood.
>         
>     - **Healing the Blind and Lepers:** Cured those born blind and cleansed lepers by Allah's permission in an era famous for medicine.
>         
>     - **Raising the Dead:** Brought deceased individuals back to life by the leave of Allah.
>         
>     - **Creating Birds from Clay:** Molded the shape of a bird out of clay, breathed into it, and it became a living bird by Allah's command.
>         
>     - **The Heavenly Table (_Al-Ma'idah_):** Caused a table spread with food to descend from heaven for his disciples.
>         
> - **Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — Given the Quran**
>     
>     - **The Holy Quran:** The ultimate, living, and eternal miracle—an unmatchable linguistic, scientific, and spiritual masterpiece revealed to an unlettered prophet.
>         
>     - **Splitting of the Moon (_Inshaqaq al-Qamar_):** The moon split into two distinct parts over the hills of Makkah as a clear visual sign for the Quraysh.
>         
>     - **Isra and Mi'raj (The Night Journey):** Traveled in a single night from Makkah to Jerusalem (_Al-Aqsa_), and subsequently ascended through the seven heavens to meet Allah.
>         
>     - **Water Gushing from Fingers:** During expeditions, water flowed from between his fingers to provide ablution and drinking water for hundreds of companions.
>         
>     - **Food Multiplication:** Small portions of food were blessed to feed entire armies during battles such as the Battle of the Trench (_Khandaq_).

## ANGLES : 

> [!NOTE] ANGELS :
> - **Jibreel (Gabriel) — The Angel of Revelation**
>     
>     - **Role:** Conveys divine revelations, scriptures, and commands from Allah to all prophets and messengers.
>         
>     - **Significance:** Brought down the Quran to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and was responsible for delivering divine messages to previous prophets (Musa, Isa, Ibrahim).
>         
> - **Mika'eel (Michael) — The Angel of Sustenance and Nature**
>     
>     - **Role:** Oversees the forces of nature, weather patterns, rain, and the distribution of provision (sustenance) to all living creations on Earth.
>         
> - **Israfeel (Raphael) — The Angel of the Trumpet**
>     
>     - **Role:** Assigned to blow the massive Horn (_Soor_) to signal the end of the world (the Day of Resurrection) and blow it a second time to resurrect all souls for judgment.
>         
> - **Malak al-Mawt (Azrael) — The Angel of Death**
>     
>     - **Role:** Responsible for taking the souls of human beings at their appointed time of death.
