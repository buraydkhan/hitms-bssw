# QUESTIONS

> [!QUESTION] Q1: Speed Conversion (120 km/h to miles/hour)
> - **Calculation:** $\frac{120}{1.609} \approx 74.58048...$

> [!QUESTION] Q2: Silver Density Conversion ($10.5\text{ g/cm}^3$ to $\text{oz/in}^3$)
> - **Calculation:**
>     $$\text{Density} = \frac{10.5\text{ g}}{1\text{ cm}^3} \times \frac{1\text{ oz}}{28.35\text{ g}} \times \frac{16.39\text{ cm}^3}{1\text{ in}^3} = \frac{10.5 \times 16.39}{28.35} = \frac{172.095}{28.35} \approx 6.07037$$
> 

> [!QUESTION] Q3: Coffee Price Conversion (3.50 USD/oz to JPY/kg)
> - **Unit Alignment Check:** To convert from $\frac{\text{USD}}{\text{oz}}$ to $\frac{\text{JPY}}{\text{kg}}$, the ounces factor must cancel out in the denominator:
>     $$\text{Cost} = \frac{3.50\text{ USD}}{1\text{ oz}} \times \frac{77.4\text{ JPY}}{1\text{ USD}} \times \frac{16\text{ oz}}{1\text{ lb}} \times \frac{2.20\text{ lb}}{1\text{ kg}}$$
> - **Calculation:**
>     $$3.50 \times 77.4 \times 16 \times 2.20 = 9,535.68\text{ JPY/kg}$$
